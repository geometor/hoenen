# De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis

**Petrus Hubertus Jacobus Hoenen, S.J.**  
*Analecta Gregoriana*, Vol. LXIII, Series Facultatis Philosophicae, Sectio A (n. 5)  
Romae: Apud Aedes Universitatis Gregorianae, 1954.

## Overview

This repository contains the critical digital edition, Latin transcription, and English translation of Father Peter Hoenen's landmark 1954 treatise, ***De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis*** (*On the Noetics of Geometry: Origin of the Theory of Knowledge*).

Published by the Pontifical Gregorian University in Rome, Hoenen's work investigates the epistemological foundation of mathematics and geometry. Bridging classical Aristotelian-Thomistic philosophy and modern 20th-century mathematical physics, Hoenen examines:
- The genesis and psychological-intellectual origin of primary mathematical notions (*punctum*, *linea*, *superficies*, *extensio*).
- The twin foundational problems: the **problem of necessity** (*problema necessitatis*) and the **problem of exactitude** (*problema exactitudinis*).
- The critique of Euclid's Fifth Postulate and the emergence of Non-Euclidean geometries (Lobachevsky, Riemann, Klein, Poincaré).
- Formal axiomatics (David Hilbert) versus Aristotelian demonstrative science (*Analytica Posteriora*).
- The ontological and noetic status of space, physical extension, motion, and time (chronotopes, Einstein's relativity).
- The doctrine of **intuitive formal abstraction** (*abstractio formalis intuitiva*), whereby the mind perceives necessary intelligible nexuses directly within imaginative data (*in phantasmate*), establishing geometry as the true origin of general epistemology (*origo noeticae generalis*).

## Assembly & Transcription Methodology

This project employs a collaborative pair-programming approach between a human scholar/archivist and an autonomous AI coding assistant (Antigravity). Together, we developed an agentic workflow designed for historical and philosophical scholarly texts.

### 1. Direct Multimodal Inspection vs. Automated OCR

Traditional Optical Character Recognition (OCR) engines (such as Tesseract) routinely fail on mid-20th-century scholarly monographs of this nature:
- **Polyglot typography**: Hoenen fluently weaves classical Latin scholastic terminology, ancient Greek citations with polytonic diacritics (e.g., `τὰ ἐξ ἀφαιρέσεως`, `ποιοῦντες γιγνώσκουσιν`, `νοῦς`), French citations (Meyerson, Poincaré), German (Hilbert, Kant), and Italian (Enriques). OCR pipelines mangle Greek characters and corrupt Latin case endings.
- **Typographical nuances**: Distinction between Roman, italics, small caps, typographic quotes (`« ... »`), and em-dashes carries vital syntactic and semantic meaning.
- **Physical degradation & scanner clipping**: Tightly bound volumes curve sharply at the inner gutter. Flatbed scanning software often clips 1–2 cm of text on odd or even pages or miscalculates crop borders due to gutter curvature.

**Our Agentic Solution**:
Instead of relying on automated OCR, the AI assistant **directly inspects every high-resolution page image** (300 DPI) using native vision capabilities (`view_file`). Each word, mark of punctuation, Greek lemma, mathematical formula, and citation is transcribed with human-level paleographic attentiveness.

### 2. Physical Scanning Logistics & Pair Troubleshooting

Working with a rare physical copy of *Analecta Gregoriana* presented significant digitization challenges:
- **Gutter Loss in Spread Scans**: In the initial scans (`chapter-X.pdf`), several even-numbered pages (e.g., pp. 212, 216, 222, 226, 230, etc.) suffered from left-margin cutoff where lines plunged into the spine.
- **Iterative Rescanning (`page-fills-2.pdf`)**: The human collaborator rescanned problematic pages, providing high-contrast two-page spreads.
- **Automated Gutter Splitting (`split_scans.py`)**: We authored specialized image processing scripts using `Pillow` to detect rotation angles, calculate gutter centers, and separate spreads into clean, individual page PNGs (`page-NNN.png`).
- **Smartphone Photographic Fallback**: When automated flatbed edge-detection persistently clipped page 224, the human collaborator provided a direct overhead camera photograph. The AI agent cropped, rotated, and transcribed the full text directly from the photograph.

## Textual Engineering & Compilation Pipeline

To preserve maximum auditability and scholarly rigor, transcription and document compilation are decoupled into two distinct stages:

```
[Raw Book Scans] (PDF / PNG)
       │
       ▼  (Direct Multimodal Inspection)
[Per-Page Transcripts] (chapter-XX/page-NNN.txt)
       │
       ▼  (Deterministic Python Pipeline: docsrc/build_caput_X.py)
[Compiled Critical Edition] (docs/caput-X.md)
```

### 1. Unit Transcriptions (`chapter-XX/page-NNN.txt`)
Each scanned page has a strictly corresponding text file containing the raw, unedited transcription of that physical page.
- Running headers (e.g., `CAPUT VII`, `DE EXTENSIONE UT EST MATERIA INTELLIGIBILIS`) and page numbers are removed.
- Publisher signature marks at page bottoms (e.g., `16 — P. HOENEN, S. I. - De Noetica Geometriae`) are discarded.
- Footnotes on the page are marked with local identifiers.
- Paragraph boundaries within the page are delimited with blank lines (`\n\n`).

### 2. Depagination & Dehyphenation
Assembling continuous prose from individual physical pages requires solving the problem of page turns:
- **Hyphenated Page Boundaries (`HYPHEN`)**: Words broken by a hyphen across the page transition (e.g., `per-` on p. 226 and `fectam` on p. 227; `al-` on p. 231 and `terius` on p. 232; `« intensita-` on p. 243 and `tis »` on p. 244) are automatically joined as single uninterrupted tokens (`perfectam`, `alterius`, `« intensitatis »`).
- **Continuous Sentences (`SAME_PARA`)**: Sentences running unbroken across the bottom of a page to the top of the next without a hyphen are merged into a single paragraph with a single normalized space.
- **True Paragraph Breaks (`NEW_PARA`)**: Page boundaries that end with a sentence terminator and are followed on the next page by an indented paragraph are preserved as distinct markdown paragraphs.
- Zero dangling hyphens are allowed in compiled output.

### 3. Footnote Management
In mid-century scholarly printing, footnotes were printed at the foot of each page, often resetting numbering to `1` on each page or running across spreads.
- Footnote calls in the body text are standardized to Markdown reference syntax (`[^1]`, `[^2]`, etc.).
- The footnote definitions are extracted from page bottoms, dehyphenated, stripped from the reading flow, and consolidated into an end-of-chapter scholarly apparatus separated by a horizontal rule (`---`).
- Every footnote reference in the text is validated to have exactly one matching definition at the end of the chapter.

### 4. Normalization of Heading Hierarchy
The printed book employed varying typesetting styles for sections: uppercase headers, bold run-in Roman numbers, parenthetical letters, and italic headings. We standardize these into a clean Markdown header hierarchy:
- `# CAPUT N` (Document Title)
- `DE TITULO CAPITIS` (Sub-title)
- `## § N.` (Major Section)
- `### N.` (Sub-section)
- `#### A)` / `*a)*` (Detailed subdivisions and run-in propositions)
