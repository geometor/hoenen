Philosophical & Epistemological Works of Petrus Hubertus Jacobus Hoenen, S.J. (1880–1961)

Digital critical editions, multi-track parallel translations, and prosopographical, bibliographic, and technical glossary apparatus for the works of Fr. Works (Opera)¶

De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis (1954)¶

Analecta Gregoriana, Vol. LXIII, Series Facultatis Philosophicae, Sectio A (n. 5). Romae: Apud Aedes Universitatis Gregorianae, 1954.

Hoenen’s crowning epistemological treatise investigating the origin of geometric knowledge through Aristotelian-Thomistic intuitive formal abstraction from the phantasm (abstractio formalis intuitiva and materia intelligibilis) in dialogue with modern axiomatics, non-Euclidean geometry, and the foundations of mathematics.

  • Synchronized Book Codex Tracks: Conspectus Operis · Praefatio (English) · Praefatio (Latin) · Caput I–IX (English & Latin) with track-preserving navigation across all chapters and appendices.

  • Scholarly Apparatus: Analytical Index (English) · Index Analyticus (Latin) · Systematic Bibliography · Index of Persons · Scholastic & Mathematical Glossary with interactive :cite:, :person:, and :term: hover cards.
