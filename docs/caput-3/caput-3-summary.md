## Executive Summary

In Chapter 3, Father Petrus Hoenen confronts the second foundational crisis of geometry: the **problem of exactitude** (*problema exactitudinis*). While arithmetic exactitude is immediate, geometric science claims absolute, infinite precision (**unextended points, widthless lines, depthless surfaces, and infinite divisibility**), despite the fact that all sensory perception and imaginative phantasms are irremediably inexact. Modern formalists declared points to be **arbitrary fictions** postulated by the will, while empiricists reduced geometry to rough physical approximations.

Hoenen demonstrates that exact indivisibles are neither fictions nor arbitrary conventions, but **necessary ontological boundaries** derived through intellective division of **extended being** (*ens extensum ut extensum*). Because a boundary separating two contiguous regions cannot itself possess thickness without becoming a third intermediate part, the intellect grasps that surfaces, lines, and points exist with **strict mathematical exactitude** as real potentialities within **intelligible matter**. Through this deduction of indivisibles and the refutation of modern objections (including the unilateral Möbius strip and pathological curves), Hoenen vindicates geometry as a **constructive science** rooted in **Aristotelian-Thomistic abstraction**.

## Thematic Glossary of Key Terms

- **Limes (Boundary / Limit)**: The limit generated by division of extended being; extended alongside the parts (*iuxta partes*), but strictly indivisible between the parts (*inter partes*).
- **Indivisibile Positionem Habens**: The classical definition of a geometric point: an entity having position in extension but possessing zero dimensions.
- **Ens Extensum ut Extensum**: Extended being considered strictly under its formal ratio of extension, serving as the fundamental subject of geometry.
- **In Potentia vs. In Actu**: Boundaries exist potentially in undivided extension and become actualized upon intellective or physical division.
- **Infinitum Pertransiri Nequit**: Classical axiom stating that an actual infinity of operations cannot be traversed in finite time, refuting pathological non-differentiable curves as completed geometric figures.
- **Constructive Science**: The realization that geometric construction is the intellective actualization of objective potentialities inherent in intelligible matter.
