# Chapter 2: On the Problem of Necessity

## Executive Summary

In Chapter 2, Father Petrus Hoenen achieves one of the decisive breakthroughs of *De Noetica Geometriae*: the **complete decoupling of the problem of necessity from the problem of exactitude**, followed by the resolution of necessity through classical Aristotelian-Thomistic epistemology. Paralyzed by the modern dogma that **"no necessary judgment can originate from sensory experience,"** modern philosophy divided into **Kantian apriorism**, **Mill's empiricist skepticism**, and **formalist nominalism**. Through direct phenomenological verifications (*constatationes*), Hoenen proves that this dogma is false: there exists a vast domain of human cognition where judgments originate in sensory experience yet yield **apodictic, unconditional necessity**.

Drawing upon **arithmetic specimens** (such as prime factorization and basic addition) and **topological geometric intuitions** (including divisibility, betweenness order, and the sectioning of the Möbius strip), Hoenen reveals that the intellect does not copy sensory contingency, but intuits an intrinsic **formal nexus** (*nexus formalis*) in **intelligible matter** (*materia intelligibilis*). By **formal abstraction**, the mind grasps necessary properties that flow inevitably from extended being as such, establishing that sensory data provide the concrete **motive and determinant** (*motivum seu determinativum*) while the intellect directly perceives unconditional necessity.

## Thematic Glossary of Key Terms

- **Constatatio**: Direct, indisputable ascertainment or phenomenological verification of a fact of consciousness.
- **Passio Propria (ἴδιον)**: A necessary property that flows universally from the essence of a subject (predication *quarto modo dicendi per se*), e.g., divisibility flowing from extension.
- **Nexus Formalis vs. Nexus Materialis**: Essential, intrinsic connection grounded in the nature signified by the subject vs. accidental coexistence of two forms in the same individual.
- **Materia Intelligibilis (ὕλη νοητή)**: Continuous spatial extension and multitude isolated from sensible qualities (color, temperature) via formal abstraction.
- **In Actu Exercito vs. In Actu Signato**: Operative in lived, implicit cognitive performance vs. explicitly formulated as formal propositional principles.
- **Motivum seu Determinativum**: The sensory phantasm acting as determinant that specifies the content of mathematical judgments, while the intellect intuits its apodictic necessity.
