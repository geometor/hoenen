=======
mission
=======

``geometor.hoenen`` is dedicated to recovering, translating, and annotating Peter Hoenen S.J.'s landmark philosophical treatise *De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis* (Analecta Gregoriana, Vol. LXIV, Rome, 1954).

Our mission is to make Hoenen's rigorous Aristotelian-Thomistic investigation of geometric intuition, the *phantasma*, and mathematical abstraction accessible alongside Euclid's *Elements* and modern constructive geometry.
