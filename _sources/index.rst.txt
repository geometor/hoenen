==================
Peter Hoenen, S.J.
==================

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Peter Hoenen, S.J. <hoenen>` (1880–1961), Professor of Natural Philosophy at the Pontifical Gregorian University whose *De Noetica Geometriae* explored the epistemological foundations of geometry through Thomistic intuitive formal abstraction and modern axiomatics.
