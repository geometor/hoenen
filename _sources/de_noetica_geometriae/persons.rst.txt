Index Nominum (Biographical Register)
=====================================

.. rubric:: * Catalogus Auctorum, Philosophorum et Mathematicorum *

This biographical register catalogues all **83 philosophers, mathematicians, physicists, and classical commentators** referenced across **Father Peter Hoenen, S.J.**'s *De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis* (1954). Every ``:person:`` reference in both the Latin and English editions links directly to its entry below, and each entry provides biographical context, cited works linked to the :ref:`Systematic Bibliography <bibliography>`, and exact back-links (`LA` · `EN`) to every occurrence in the text.

Albert the Great, St., O.P. (c. 1200 – 1280)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Alfred Edward Taylor (1869 – 1945)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Role**: *British Idealist Philosopher & Plato Scholar*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Professor of Moral Philosophy at St Andrews and Edinburgh, renowned for his commentary on Plato's Timaeus and comprehensive study of Plato's dialogues.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`View Full Entry in Systematic Bibliography <bib-taylor-alfred-edward>`

.. _person-whitehead:

Alfred North Whitehead (1861 – 1947)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Role**: *British Mathematician & Process Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Co-author with Bertrand Russell of Principia Mathematica and creator of the method of extensive abstraction for deriving geometric points and instants from overlapping physical events.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`View Full Entry in Systematic Bibliography <bib-whitehead-alfred-north>`
* **Principal Works**: *Principia Mathematica (with B. Russell, 1910–1913)* · *A Treatise on Universal Algebra (1898)* · *An Enquiry Concerning the Principles of Natural Knowledge (1919)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (2)**: **Caput V**: A) (`LA <caput-5/caput-5.html#occ-person-whitehead-1>`__ · `EN <caput-5/caput-5-en.html#occ-person-whitehead-1>`__), B) (`LA <caput-5/caput-5.html#occ-person-whitehead-2>`__ · `EN <caput-5/caput-5-en.html#occ-person-whitehead-2>`__)
