Themistius
==========

* **Role**: *Late Antique Rhetorician & Aristotelian Commentator*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Composed lucid paraphrases of Aristotle's Posterior Analytics, Physics, and De Anima that were widely studied by Arabic and Latin Scholastics.
