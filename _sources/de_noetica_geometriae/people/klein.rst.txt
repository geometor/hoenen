Felix Klein
===========

* **Role**: *German Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Author of the Erlangen Program classifying geometries by transformation groups, and originator of the philosophical distinction between Approximationsmathematik (empirical perception bounded by a threshold) and Präzisionsmathematik (idealized limits).
