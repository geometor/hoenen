Sir Isaac Newton
================

* **Role**: *English Mathematician & Natural Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Author of the Principia Mathematica and co-founder of the calculus (method of fluxions); defended absolute space and time and the kinematic generation of geometric magnitudes.
