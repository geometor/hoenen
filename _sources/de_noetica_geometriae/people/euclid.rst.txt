Euclid of Alexandria
====================

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Geometer*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Author of the Elements, whose tripartite division of first principles into Definitions (ὅροι), Postulates (αἰτήματα), and Common Notions (κοιναὶ ἔννοιαι) embodies the Aristotelian demonstrative ideal.
