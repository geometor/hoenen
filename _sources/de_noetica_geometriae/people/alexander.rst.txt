Alexander of Aphrodisias
========================

* **Role**: *Peripatetic Commentator*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Foremost ancient Greek commentator on Aristotle, known in antiquity and the Middle Ages simply as 'The Commentator' before Averroes.
