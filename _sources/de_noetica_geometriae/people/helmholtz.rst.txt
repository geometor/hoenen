Hermann von Helmholtz
=====================

* **Role**: *German Physicist, Physiologist & Philosopher of Science*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Investigated the physiological optics of spatial perception and characterized physical geometry through the free mobility of rigid bodies (Riemann-Helmholtz space problem).
