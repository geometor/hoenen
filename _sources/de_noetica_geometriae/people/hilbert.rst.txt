David Hilbert
=============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician & Leader of Formalism*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Author of Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899), which replaced intuitive definitions with implicit axiomatic characterization of incidence, order, congruence, parallels, and continuity.
