Anicius Manlius Severinus Boethius
==================================

* **Role**: *Roman Christian Philosopher & Translator*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Late antique philosopher whose treatise De Trinitate occasioned Thomas Aquinas's celebrated commentary on the division and methods of the speculative sciences.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`View Full Entry in Systematic Bibliography <bib-thomas-aquinas-st-o-p>`
* **Principal Works**: *De Trinitate* · *De Institutione Arithmetica* · *De Consolatione Philosophiae*
