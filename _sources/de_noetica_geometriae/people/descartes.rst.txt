René Descartes
==============

* **Role**: *French Philosopher & Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of analytic geometry and modern rationalism; Hoenen critically examines both his coordinate reduction of extension and the intuitive nature of his Cogito ergo sum.
