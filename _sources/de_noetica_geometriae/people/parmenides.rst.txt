Parmenides of Elea
==================

* **Role**: *Pre-Socratic Eleatic Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of the Eleatic school who affirmed the unity and immutability of Being and denied the reality of motion and division.
* **Principal Works**: *On Nature (Fragments)*
