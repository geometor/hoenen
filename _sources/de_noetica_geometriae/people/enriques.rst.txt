Federigo Enriques
=================

* **Role**: *Italian Geometer & Philosopher of Science*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Leader of the Italian school of algebraic geometry and editor of Questioni riguardanti le matematiche elementari, who analyzed the psychological and sensory origins of geometric concepts.
