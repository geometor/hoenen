Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz
=========================

* **Role**: *German Polymath, Philosopher & Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Co-inventor of the infinitesimal calculus and pioneer of symbolic logic (characteristica universalis) and analysis situs, who sought to reduce all mathematical truths to identities demonstrable via definitions.
