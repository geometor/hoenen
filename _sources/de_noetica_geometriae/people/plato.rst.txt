Plato
=====

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of the Athenian Academy; his Divided Line (Republic VI) assigned mathematical objects to discursive understanding (διάνοια) proceeding from hypotheses with the aid of visible diagrams.
