Albert Einstein
===============

* **Role**: *Theoretical Physicist*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Creator of special and general relativity; his 1921 lecture 'Geometrie und Erfahrung' famously distinguished purely axiomatic geometry from practical physical geometry.
