Gerhard Stammler
================

* **Role**: *German Philosopher of Mathematics & Logic*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Neo-Kantian philosopher at Halle who analyzed the history of the number concept since Gauss and the logical theory of concepts, judgments, and inferences.
