Georg Cantor
============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician & Creator of Set Theory*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of transfinite set theory (Mengenlehre) and the arithmetic of the continuum, whose definitions of point-sets and continuity transformed modern analysis.
