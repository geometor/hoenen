Sir Thomas Little Heath
=======================

* **Role**: *British Historian of Ancient Greek Mathematics*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Foremost English translator and commentator of Euclid, Archimedes, and Apollonius, whose monumental commentary on Euclid's Elements is a primary source for Hoenen.
