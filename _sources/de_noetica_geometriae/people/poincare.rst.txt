Henri Poincaré
==============

* **Role**: *French Mathematician, Physicist & Philosopher of Science*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Universal mathematician and creator of algebraic topology (analysis situs) who argued that geometric axioms are neither synthetic a priori judgments nor experimental facts, but conventions (disguised definitions) guided by experience.
