Petrus Hubertus Jacobus Hoenen, S.J.
====================================

* **Role**: *Jesuit Philosopher of Science & Mathematics*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Professor of Cosmology and Philosophy of Mathematics at the Pontifical Gregorian University in Rome; author of De Noetica Geometriae and La théorie du jugement d'après St. Thomas d'Aquin.
