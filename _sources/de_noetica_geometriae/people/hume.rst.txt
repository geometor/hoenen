David Hume
==========

* **Role**: *Scottish Empiricist Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Radical empiricist who attacked the exactness of Euclidean geometry in the Treatise by reducing space to discrete sensory minima (minimum visibile) incapable of infinite divisibility.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`View Full Entry in Systematic Bibliography <bib-hume-david>`
