Giuseppe Peano
==============

* **Role**: *Italian Mathematician & Logician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Formulated the Peano axioms for arithmetic, symbolic mathematical logic, and the space-filling Peano curve (1890) challenging naive intuitions of dimension.
* **Principal Works**: *Arithmetices principia, nova methodo exposita (1889)* · *I principii di geometria logicamente esposti (1889)* · *Sur une courbe, qui remplit toute une aire plane (1890)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1)**: **Caput II**: C) (`LA <caput-2/caput-2.html#occ-person-peano-1>`__ · `EN <caput-2/caput-2-en.html#occ-person-peano-1>`__)
