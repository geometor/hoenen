Pythagoras of Samos
===================

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Philosopher & Geometer*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of the Pythagorean brotherhood, which discovered the mathematical ratios of musical harmony, the Pythagorean theorem, and the incommensurability of the diagonal with the side.
* **Principal Works**: *Pythagorean Tradition*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1)**: **Caput IV**: n. 8 (`LA <caput-4/caput-4.html#occ-person-pythagoras-1>`__ · `EN <caput-4/caput-4-en.html#occ-person-pythagoras-1>`__)
