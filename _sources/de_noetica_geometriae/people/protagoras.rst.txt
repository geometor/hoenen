Protagoras of Abdera
====================

* **Role**: *Greek Sophist*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Sophist who argued against geometers that sensible circles and rulers touch at more than a single indivisible point (reported by Aristotle, Metaphysics III 2, 997b35).
* **Principal Works**: *Fragments (Diels-Kranz)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1)**: **Caput III**: n. 1 (`LA <caput-3/caput-3.html#occ-person-protagoras-1>`__ · `EN <caput-3/caput-3-en.html#occ-person-protagoras-1>`__)
