Eudoxus of Cnidus
=================

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Mathematician & Astronomer*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Member of Plato's Academy who created the general theory of proportions (Elements Book V) and the method of exhaustion (Book XII) to resolve the crisis of incommensurables.
* **Principal Works**: *Theory of Proportions (preserved in Euclid, Elements V)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (2)**: **Caput I**: § 1. (`LA <caput-1/caput-1.html#occ-person-eudoxus-1>`__ · `EN <caput-1/caput-1-en.html#occ-person-eudoxus-1>`__), § 4. (`LA <caput-1/caput-1.html#occ-person-eudoxus-2>`__ · `EN <caput-1/caput-1-en.html#occ-person-eudoxus-2>`__)
