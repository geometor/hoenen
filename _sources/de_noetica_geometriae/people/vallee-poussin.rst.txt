Charles-Jean de la Vallée Poussin
=================================

* **Role**: *Belgian Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Professor at Louvain who proved the Prime Number Theorem independently of Hadamard and authored the classic Cours d'Analyse Infinitésimale.
* **Principal Works**: *Cours d'Analyse Infinitésimale (1903)*
