Simplicius of Cilicia
=====================

* **Role**: *Neoplatonic Commentator on Aristotle*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Student of Ammonius and Damascius whose encyclopedic commentaries on Aristotle's Physics and De Caelo preserve crucial fragments of Eudemus, Hippocrates of Chios, and ancient geometry.
