Eugenio Beltrami
================

* **Role**: *Italian Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Pioneer of differential geometry who constructed the first concrete Euclidean models (pseudosphere) proving the relative consistency of non-Euclidean geometry.
* **Principal Works**: *Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea (1868)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1)**: **Caput IV**: § 5. (`LA <caput-4/caput-4.html#occ-person-beltrami-1>`__ · `EN <caput-4/caput-4-en.html#occ-person-beltrami-1>`__)
