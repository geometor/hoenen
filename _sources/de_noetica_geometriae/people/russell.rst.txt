Bertrand Russell
================

* **Role**: *British Philosopher, Logician & Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Co-author of Principia Mathematica and leading proponent of logicism, who defined pure mathematics as the class of all formal implications 'p implies q' containing only logical constants and variables.
