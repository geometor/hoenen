Hans Reichenbach
================

* **Role**: *German Philosopher of Science & Logical Empiricist*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of the Berlin Circle who analyzed relativistic spacetime geometry via 'coordinative definitions' (Zuordnungsdefinitionen) and critiqued geometric visualization.
