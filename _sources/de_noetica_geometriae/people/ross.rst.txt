Sir William David Ross
======================

* **Role**: *Scottish Philosopher & Aristotle Scholar*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Provost of Oriel College, Oxford, and general editor of the Oxford Translation of Aristotle; his critical editions and commentaries on Aristotle's Metaphysics, Physics, and Analytics are frequently cited by Hoenen.
