Ferdinand Gonseth
=================

* **Role**: *Swiss Mathematician & Philosopher of Science*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Professor at ETH Zürich who characterized mathematical objects as idealized 'schematic physics' (physique de l'objet quelconque) abstracted from experimental reality.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`View Full Entry in Systematic Bibliography <bib-gonseth-ferdinand>`
* **Principal Works**: *Les fondements des mathématiques (1926)* · *Les mathématiques et la réalité (1936)*
