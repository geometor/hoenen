August Ferdinand Möbius
=======================

* **Role**: *German Mathematician & Astronomer*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Pioneer of projective geometry and topology; discovered the non-orientable one-sided surface (folium Moebii / Möbius strip), which Hoenen analyzes in detail in Caput III.
* **Principal Works**: *Der barycentrische Calcul (1827)* · *Über die Bestimmung des Inhaltes eines Polyëders (1865)*
