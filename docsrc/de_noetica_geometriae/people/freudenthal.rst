Hans Freudenthal
================

* **Role**: *German-Dutch Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Topologist and historian of mathematics who engaged directly with Hoenen in Gregorianum on the roles of intuition and abstraction in the foundations of geometry.
