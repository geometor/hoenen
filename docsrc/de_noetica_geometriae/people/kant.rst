Immanuel Kant
=============

* **Role**: *German Critical Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Characterized geometric propositions as synthetic a priori judgments grounded in pure intuition of space as an a priori form of sensibility—a doctrine Hoenen contrasts with Aristotelian abstraction from sensible phantasms.
