Gerhard Hessenberg
==================

* **Role**: *German Mathematician & Neo-Friesian Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Geometer and foundational theorist known for his work on projective geometry (Desargues's and Pascal's theorems) and philosophical critique of mathematical axiomatics.
