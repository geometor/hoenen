Theaetetus of Athens
====================

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Associate of Plato's Academy who classified quadratic irrationals (Euclid, Elements Book X) and completed the construction of the five regular polyhedra (Book XIII).
* **Principal Works**: *Theory of Irrationals & Regular Solids (in Euclid, Elements X & XIII)*
* **Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1)**: **Caput I**: § 1. (`LA <caput-1/caput-1.html#occ-person-theaetetus-1>`__ · `EN <caput-1/caput-1-en.html#occ-person-theaetetus-1>`__)
