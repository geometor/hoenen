Moritz Pasch
============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Pioneered the rigorous axiomatization of projective and order geometry (Pasch's Axiom), insisting that geometric deduction must proceed independently of intuitive diagrams once nuclear axioms (Kern-Sätze) are abstracted from experience.
