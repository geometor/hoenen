John Locke
==========

* **Role**: *English Empiricist Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Author of An Essay Concerning Human Understanding, whose conceptualist account of abstract general ideas was critiqued by Berkeley, Hume, and Leibniz.
