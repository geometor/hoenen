John Philoponus
===============

* **Role**: *Alexandrian Christian Philosopher & Aristotelian Commentator*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Late antique commentator on Aristotle's Physics and Posterior Analytics who developed the theory of three-dimensional spatial extension (διάστημα) and impetus.
