Luitzen Egbertus Jan Brouwer
============================

* **Role**: *Dutch Mathematician & Founder of Intuitionism*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Topologist and philosopher of mathematics who founded mathematical intuitionism, grounding mathematics in the primordial intuition of time rather than formalist axioms.
