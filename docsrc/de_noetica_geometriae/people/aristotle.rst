Aristotle
=========

* **Role**: *Ancient Greek Philosopher (The Stagirite)*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Founder of the Peripatetic school and formal logic, whose theory of demonstrative science (Analytica Posteriora), abstraction (τὰ ἐξ ἀφαιρέσεως), and intelligible matter (ὕλη νοητή) originates classical geometric epistemology.
