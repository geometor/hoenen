Helmut Hasse
============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Algebraist and number theorist who co-authored with Heinrich Scholz a celebrated treatise on the foundational crisis of Greek mathematics triggered by incommensurability.

* **Principal Works**: *Die Grundlagenkrisis der griechischen Mathematik (with H. Scholz, 1928)*
