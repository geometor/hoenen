Louis Couturat
==============

* **Role**: *French Logician & Philosopher of Mathematics*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Champion of symbolic logic and Russellian logicism in France, editor of Leibniz's unpublished logical manuscripts, and critic of Kantian mathematical intuition.
