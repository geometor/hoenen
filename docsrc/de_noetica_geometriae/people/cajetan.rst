Thomas de Vio Cajetan, Cardinal, O.P.
=====================================

* **Role**: *Renaissance Thomistic Commentator*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Master General of the Dominicans and classic commentator on the Summa Theologiae, who systematized the distinction between abstractio formalis and abstractio totalis.
* **Bibliographic Apparatus**: :ref:`Systematic Bibliography <bibliography>`
* **Principal Works**: *In De Ente et Essentia Commentaria (1495)* · *Commentaria in Summam Theologiae*
