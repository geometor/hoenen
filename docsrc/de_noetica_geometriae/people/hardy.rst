Godfrey Harold Hardy
====================

* **Role**: *British Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Leading Cambridge analyst and number theorist whose 1929 essay 'Mathematical Proof' defended genuine intellectual insight against purely formalist symbol-games.
