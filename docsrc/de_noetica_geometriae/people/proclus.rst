Proclus Lycius Diadochus
========================

* **Role**: *Neoplatonic Philosopher & Commentator on Euclid*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Head of the Athenian Neoplatonic Academy whose Commentary on Book I of Euclid's Elements is the primary ancient source on the history and philosophy of Greek geometry and imagination (φαντασία) as passive intellect.
