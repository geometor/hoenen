George Berkeley
===============

* **Role**: *Anglo-Irish Empiricist Philosopher*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Bishop of Cloyne whose critique of abstract ideas and theory of sensory minima (minimum visibile) prepared Hume's radical empiricism.
* **Principal Works**: *A Treatise Concerning the Principles of Human Knowledge (1710)* · *An Essay Towards a New Theory of Vision (1709)*
