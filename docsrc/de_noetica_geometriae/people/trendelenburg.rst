Friedrich Adolf Trendelenburg
=============================

* **Role**: *German Philosopher & Aristotle Scholar*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Leader of the nineteenth-century Aristotelian revival in Germany, editor of De Anima, and critic of Hegelian dialectic and Kantian subjectivity.
* **Principal Works**: *Logische Untersuchungen (1840)* · *Elementa logices Aristoteleae (1836)*
