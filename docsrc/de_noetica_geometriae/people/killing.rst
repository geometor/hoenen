Wilhelm Killing
===============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Co-discoverer of Lie algebras (Killing form) and professor at Münster who wrote a comprehensive two-volume treatise on the foundations of Euclidean and non-Euclidean geometry.
