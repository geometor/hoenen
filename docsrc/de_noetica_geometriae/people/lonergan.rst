Bernard Lonergan, S.J.
======================

* **Role**: *Jesuit Philosopher & Theologian*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Studied under Hoenen at the Gregorian University; his Verbum articles (1946–1949) and Insight (1957) developed Hoenen's discovery of Aquinas's doctrine of intellectual insight into phantasms.
