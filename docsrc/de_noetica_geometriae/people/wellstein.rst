Josef Wellstein
===============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Professor at Strasbourg and co-author with Heinrich Weber of the Enzyklopädie der Elementarmathematik, whose chapters on the foundations of geometry are extensively analyzed by Hoenen.
