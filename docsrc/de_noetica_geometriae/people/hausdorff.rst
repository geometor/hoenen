Felix Hausdorff
===============

* **Role**: *German Mathematician & Founder of General Topology*
* **Biographical & Philosophical Note**: Creator of the theory of topological and metric spaces (Hausdorff spaces) and author of philosophical essays on the problem of physical and geometric space.
