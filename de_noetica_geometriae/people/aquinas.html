St. Thomas Aquinas, O.P.¶

  • Role: Scholastic Philosopher & Theologian (Doctor Angelicus)

  • Biographical & Philosophical Note: Dominican friar and Doctor of the Church whose synthesis of Aristotelian noetics—abstraction from phantasms, intelligible matter, and judgment by resolution to sensory principles—forms the central framework of Hoenen’s treatise.
