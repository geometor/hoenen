Every inline :term: in the Latin and English editions links directly to its entry below, and each entry provides the original headword, language tradition, English translation, philosophical explanation, and direct back-links (LA · EN) to every occurrence in the text.

Glossary Entries & Textual Concordance¶

abstractio formalis — Formal abstraction¶

  • Headword: abstractio formalis (abstractio-formalis)

  • Language Tradition: Latin (Scholastic)

  • English Translation: Formal abstraction

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Abstraction of the intelligible form or mathematical nature (quantity/extension) from sensible matter.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (2): Appendix: § 1. (LA · EN), § 1. (LA · EN)

abstractio intuitiva formalis — Intuitive formal abstraction¶

  • Headword: abstractio intuitiva formalis (abstractio-intuitiva)

  • Language Tradition: Latin (Scholastic)

  • English Translation: Intuitive formal abstraction

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Hoenen’s technical designation for the intellectual operation in which the mind directly intuits universal, necessary formal relationships (such as spatial continuity and geometric properties) within the concrete sensible phantasm.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (4): Appendix: § 1. (LA · EN), § 1. (LA · EN), § 1. (LA · EN), § 7. (LA · EN)

agere sequitur ad esse in actu — Action follows upon actual being¶

  • Headword: agere sequitur ad esse in actu (agere-sequitur-esse)

  • Language Tradition: Latin (Scholastic)

  • English Translation: Action follows upon actual being

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Classical Thomistic metaphysical axiom affirming that no causal agency or operation can proceed except from an entity that already possesses actual existence (esse in actu).

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (4): Appendix: § 1. (LA · EN), § 1. (LA · EN), § 1. (LA · EN), § 3. (LA · EN)

αἴσθημα (pl. αἰσθήματα) — Sense-impression / perceptual datum¶

  • Headword: αἴσθημα (pl. αἰσθήματα) (aisthema)

  • Language Tradition: Greek

  • English Translation: Sense-impression / perceptual datum

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: In Aristotle’s De Anima (III, c. 8, 432a), the content of sensory perception from which the imagination forms phantasms and the intellect abstracts intelligible species.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1): Caput III: A) (LA · EN)

αἴτημα (αἰτήματα) — Postulate (postulates)¶

  • Headword: αἴτημα (αἰτήματα) (aitema)

  • Language Tradition: Greek

  • English Translation: Postulate (postulates)

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: In Aristotle and Euclid, a demonstrable or constructible principle requested from the learner without proof within the science.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (3): Caput I: § 1. (LA · EN), § 5. (LA · EN) | Caput IV: § 7. (LA · EN)

αἰτεῖται — He postulates / requests¶

  • Headword: αἰτεῖται (aiteo)

  • Language Tradition: Greek

  • English Translation: He postulates / requests

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Aristotle’s verb (Anal. Post. I 10, 76b28) for assuming a principle when the learner has no prior opinion or holds a contrary opinion.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1): Caput IV: § 7. (LA · EN)

ἀναιρεῖ (ἀναιρεῖν) — Destroys / sublates (hypotheses)¶

  • Headword: ἀναιρεῖ (ἀναιρεῖν) (anairei)

  • Language Tradition: Greek

  • English Translation: Destroys / sublates (hypotheses)

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Plato’s term in Republic VII (533c) for the dialectical method, which ‘destroys’ or transcends the provisional hypotheses of the mathematical sciences in order to ascend to the unhypothetical first principle.

  • Occurrences in De Noetica Geometriae (1): Caput I: § 1. (LA · EN)

  • Headword: andererseits (andererseits)

  • Language Tradition: German

  • English Translation: On the other hand

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Correlative discourse marker used in German mathematical exposition (paired with einerseits) when contrasting dual axiomatic or geometric aspects.

Anordnung — Order / Arrangement¶

  • Headword: Anordnung (anordnung)

  • Language Tradition: German

  • English Translation: Order / Arrangement

  • Noetic & Mathematical Definition: Hilbert’s second group of geometric axioms (Axioms of Order / Betweenness) in Grundlagen der Geometrie.

the apotheosis of stupidity¶

  • Headword: the apotheosis of stupidity (apotheosis-of-stupidity)

  • Language Tradition: English

  • English Translation: The apotheosis of stupidity
